Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

yt-studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own original videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It has no other users.

YouTube: yt-studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. With the owner's permission (OAuth), it uploads videos to the owner's channel and reads that channel's public data and analytics. Access can be revoked at Google security settings.

TikTok: with the owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), yt-studio reads basic profile information (user.info.basic) and uploads the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), where the owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. See the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Instagram: through the Meta Instagram API, yt-studio publishes the owner's videos as Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account. Access can be removed in Meta Business Settings. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

Storage: access tokens and API data are stored only on the owner's computer. Videos are held in private cloud storage only for the minutes a platform needs to fetch them, then deleted. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Locally stored data is deleted on request or within 30 days of access being revoked.
